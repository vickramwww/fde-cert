Product Requirements: Billing Portal v2

R1 (P0) Invoice download. Customers must be able to download any invoice from the last 24 months as PDF.
    Generation must complete in under 5 seconds for invoices up to 200 line items.
R2 (P0) Payment method management. Customers can add, remove and set a default card. Card data is handled
    exclusively by Stripe Elements; no PAN data may touch our servers (PCI SAQ-A scope).
R3 (P1) Usage alerts. Account admins can configure an email alert when monthly usage reaches 80% of plan.
R4 (P2) Multi-currency display for EUR and GBP. Display only; billing remains in USD.
R5 (P3) Export of billing history to CSV.

Out of scope: tax calculation, refunds.
